When organisations change

Too many decisions still land on the leadership team.

Senior leaders spend their time firefighting and approving, while the strategic questions wait.

What it looks like

  • Escalation is the default behaviour
  • Capable people wait for instructions
  • Strategic topics are squeezed out of leadership meetings
  • Leaders lack an independent sounding board for big moves
  • Empowerment is talked about but not felt

Why it is harder than it looks

Decision authority is rarely designed; it accumulates. Empowerment is often announced rather than built. Leaders end up carrying decisions the organisation should be able to make, and have few people to think the big ones through with.

How SC approaches it

Treat the system, not just the symptom.

SC works across the organisation, using the four dimensions of the High-Performance EXOSYSTEM™.

Define decision authority

Who decides what, at which level, with what information.

Build empowerment routines

The habits and leadership behaviours that let people act like owners.

Support the leaders themselves

A confidential, experienced thinking partner for the decisions that matter.
