Confidentiality & Data Protection

What you share with us stays with us.

Last updated: 15 November 2026Applies to: all conversations, diagnostics and engagements

Confidential from day one

Every conversation is treated as confidential, NDA or not.

Mutual NDA on request

We will sign before any detailed or sensitive discussion.

Diagnostic data protected

OED inputs and reports are used only for your organisation.

On this page
  1. Our commitment
  2. Non-disclosure agreements
  3. Diagnostic data
  4. Dialogues
  5. Client confidentiality

01Our commitment

Leaders bring SC sensitive questions about growth, acquisitions, listings, restructuring and leadership, often long before anything is public. Every conversation is treated as confidential from the first contact.

02Non-disclosure agreements

We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share detailed or commercially sensitive information, using our standard mutual NDA or yours. It covers:

Confidential informationAll business, financial, strategic, organisational and personal information shared by either party
PurposeUsed only to discuss or deliver the engagement
Non-disclosureNo disclosure to third parties, except advisers bound by confidentiality or where the law requires
Return or destructionOn request, information is returned or securely destroyed
DurationFor the period agreed in the NDA, including after the engagement ends

03Diagnostic data

Information submitted for the Organisational Effectiveness Diagnosis is used only to prepare your organisation's diagnosis and report. Where SC uses aggregated insights for research, they are anonymised so no organisation or individual can be identified.

04Dialogues

Executive Dialogues run on the principle that what is said in the room stays in the room. Private sessions are never discussed outside the client organisation.

05Client confidentiality

We do not name clients, publish case studies or use client logos without written permission.

This page summarises our approach. It is not itself a contract. Binding obligations are set out in a signed NDA or engagement agreement.
