Transformation Orchestration Lab

Strengthening leadership capability to orchestrate and sustain enterprise transformation.

Template page. Structure is final; detailed programme content is draft for SC to confirm. Every programme uses this same template.
Audience
Senior leaders and transformation teams
Format
Interactive lab
Delivery
Public or in-house
Status
On request

The challenge

Large transformations involve many functions, partners and workstreams. Without deliberate orchestration, activity grows while value lags behind.

Who it is for

  • Executives sponsoring enterprise or digital transformation
  • Transformation Office and PMO leads
  • Leaders of functions going through major change

Outcomes

Clearer orchestration

A shared view of how workstreams, partners and leadership connect.

Stronger governance

Transformation governance that drives decisions, not just reporting.

Sustained adoption

Practical ways to move from go-live to value realisation.

Themes

  • Orchestrating across functions and implementation partners
  • Transformation governance and decision rights
  • Readiness, adoption and value realisation
  • Sustaining change after the programme ends

Format and delivery

Duration, schedule and fees to be confirmed by SC. Available as a public lab or as an in-house programme customised for one organisation.
