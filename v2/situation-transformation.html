When organisations change

A major transformation is not delivering the value expected.

An ERP or digital programme, restructuring or acquisition is underway. Activity is high, but adoption and value lag behind.

What it looks like

  • The system went live, but people work around it
  • Workstreams and implementation partners are not aligned
  • Governance reports activity, not value
  • Change fatigue is setting in
  • Post-merger integration has stalled

Why it is harder than it looks

Technology and structural changes often move faster than the organisation's readiness to absorb them. Ownership is spread across functions and partners, so no one connects the effort end to end.
