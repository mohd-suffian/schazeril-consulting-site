About SC

A boutique executive and organisational advisory firm.

SC helps organisations address the conditions that create operational friction, so strategy, growth and change translate into sustained performance.

What we believeFounderCareerEducation

What we believe

Organisational performance does not happen by accident. It requires deliberate attention to how the organisation works as a whole.

Performance is engineered.

Through management systems, processes and decisions designed to work together.

Culture is shaped.

Through the routines and leadership behaviours people see every day.

Transformation is intentional.

Through clear choices about what to change, and why.

Founder and Principal Consultant

Schazeril Shamsuddin

Before founding Schazeril Consulting, Schazeril spent nearly 14 years at Petronas Chemicals Group (PCHEM), most recently as Head of the Transformation Office. He also led Design Change for the Transformation Office and headed the Operational Excellence Management System (OeXMS).

Earlier, he spent eight years as Change Leader and Organisational Effectiveness Leader at OPTIMAL Group of Companies. Today he works with CEOs and senior leadership teams to align operating models, governance and decision authority so execution becomes stable and scalable.

Selected landmark programmes (SC to supply): two or three named programmes from PCHEM and OPTIMAL, with scope and results, for example the OeXMS rollout or major transformation initiatives led.
