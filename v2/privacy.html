01Who we are

This policy is issued by Schazeril Consulting PLT, Kuala Lumpur, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (together, the PDPA).

02Data we collect

Contact detailsName, job title, company, work email and phone number
Enquiry detailsWhat you tell us about your organisation and the support you need
RegistrationsDetails you provide when registering for a Dialogue, programme or diagnosis
Engagement recordsNotes and correspondence from our conversations and sessions

03How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange conversations
  • To deliver our advisory services, Dialogues, programmes and diagnostics
  • To manage our client relationship, including proposals, agreements and invoicing
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party marketing.

04Who we share it with

Service providersWho help us run this website, registrations, payments and communications
Professional advisersSuch as lawyers and auditors, under a duty of confidentiality
AuthoritiesOnly where the law requires it

05Transfers outside Malaysia

Some service providers store data outside Malaysia. When this happens, we take steps so that your personal data receives a level of protection comparable to the PDPA.

06Cookies and analytics

This website uses analytics to understand which pages are useful. It does not use advertising cookies. Your browser also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

07Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed, or as the law requires, then securely delete or anonymise it. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act promptly and notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals where the PDPA requires.
