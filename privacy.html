Privacy Notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Notice is issued by Schazeril Consulting PLT ("we", "us") in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (together, the "PDPA"). It explains how we handle personal data you share with us through this website, by phone, by WhatsApp or in person.

1. Personal data we collect

  • Contact details: your name, job title, company, work email and phone number.
  • Enquiry details: what you tell us about your organisation and the support you are looking for.
  • Engagement records: notes and correspondence from our conversations and advisory sessions.

Name and email are required for us to respond to an enquiry. All other fields are optional. If you choose not to provide the required details, we may not be able to respond.

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange conversations.
  • To deliver our advisory and consulting services, including SC Executive Advisor.
  • To manage our client relationship, including proposals, agreements and invoicing.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party marketing.

3. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed for the purposes above, with:

  • Service providers who help us run this website and our communications, such as website hosting and form handling.
  • Professional advisers, such as our lawyers and auditors, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

4. Transfers outside Malaysia

Some of our service providers store data outside Malaysia. When this happens, we take steps so that your personal data receives a level of protection comparable to the PDPA.

5. This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you visit, your browser loads fonts and images from third-party providers (Google Fonts and Unsplash), which receive your IP address as part of normal internet traffic.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. After that, we securely delete or anonymise it.

7. How we protect it

We take practical security steps to protect personal data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access and disclosure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act on it promptly and notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals where the PDPA requires.

8. Your rights

Subject to the PDPA, you may:

  • Ask for access to your personal data, and ask us to correct it.
  • Withdraw your consent, or ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Ask us to transmit your data to another organisation (data portability), where this applies.

9. Contact us

For any question or request about your personal data, contact us by phone or WhatsApp at +6012 332 1875, or through our contact page.
