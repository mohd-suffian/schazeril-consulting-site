In short

We collect only what we need to respond to you and serve you, we keep it confidential, we never sell it, and you can ask to see, correct or withdraw it at any time.

01Who we are

This notice is issued by Schazeril Consulting PLT, Kuala Lumpur, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (together, the PDPA). It covers personal data you share with us through this website, by phone, by WhatsApp or in person.

02Data we collect

  • Contact details: name, job title, company, work email and phone number
  • Enquiry details: what you tell us about your organisation and the support you need
  • Engagement records: notes and correspondence from our conversations and sessions

Name and email are required to respond to an enquiry. Everything else is optional.

03How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange conversations
  • To deliver our advisory and consulting services, including SC Executive Advisor
  • To manage our client relationship, including proposals, agreements and invoicing
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party marketing.

04Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help us run this website and our communications
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and auditors, under a duty of confidentiality
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

05Transfers outside Malaysia

Some of our service providers store data outside Malaysia. When this happens, we take steps so that your personal data receives a level of protection comparable to the PDPA.

06This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you visit, your browser loads fonts and images from third-party providers (Google Fonts and Unsplash), which receive your IP address as part of normal internet traffic.

07Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as the law requires, and then securely delete or anonymise it. We take practical steps to protect it from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act promptly and notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals where the PDPA requires.

08Your rights

  • Ask for access to your personal data and ask us to correct it
  • Withdraw consent, or ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Ask us to transmit your data to another organisation (data portability), where this applies

09Contact us

For any question or request about your personal data, call or WhatsApp +6012 332 1875, or use our contact page.
