Who we are

Advisors who have run the systems they now design

Before founding Schazeril Consulting, Schazeril spent nearly 14 years at Petronas Chemicals Group (PCHEM), most recently as Head of the Transformation Office. He also led Design Change for the Transformation Office and headed the Operational Excellence Management System (OeXMS).

Earlier, he spent eight years as Change Leader and Organisational Effectiveness Leader at OPTIMAL Group of Companies. Today he works with CEOs and senior leadership teams to align operating models, governance and decision authority so execution becomes stable and scalable.
