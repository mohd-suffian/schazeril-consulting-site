Who we are

Two decades of making transformation hold.

Schazeril Consulting is led by Schazeril Shamsuddin, an Enterprise Operating Ecosystem Architect who has spent his career inside the organisations he now advises.

Founder

Schazeril Shamsuddin

Enterprise Operating Ecosystem Architect

Before founding Schazeril Consulting, Schazeril spent nearly 14 years at Petronas Chemicals Group (PCHEM), most recently as Head of the Transformation Office. He also led Design Change for the Transformation Office and headed the Operational Excellence Management System (OeXMS).

Earlier, he spent eight years as Change Leader and Organisational Effectiveness Leader at OPTIMAL Group of Companies.

Today he works with CEOs and senior leadership teams to design their enterprise operating ecosystem, aligning operating models, governance and decision authority so execution becomes stable and scalable.
