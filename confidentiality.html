In short

Everything you share is confidential from the first conversation, and we will sign a mutual NDA before any detailed discussion.

01Our commitment

Senior leaders bring us sensitive questions about acquisitions, listings, restructuring and leadership, often long before anything is public. Every conversation with us is treated as confidential from the first contact, whether or not an NDA is in place.

02Non-disclosure agreements

We are happy to sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share detailed or commercially sensitive information. We can use our standard mutual NDA or review yours. Our standard mutual NDA covers:

  • Confidential information: all business, financial, strategic, organisational and personal information shared by either party, in any form
  • Purpose: information is used only to discuss or deliver the engagement
  • Non-disclosure: no disclosure to third parties, except advisers bound by confidentiality or where the law requires
  • Protection: each party protects the other's information with at least the care it gives its own
  • Return or destruction: on request, information is returned or securely destroyed
  • Duration: obligations continue for the period agreed in the NDA, including after the engagement ends

03SC Executive Advisor sessions

Advisory conversations are confidential by design. We do not share what is discussed with anyone inside or outside your organisation without your consent.

04Client confidentiality

We do not name clients, publish case studies or use client logos without written permission.

05Request an NDA

To request our standard mutual NDA, or to send yours for review, call or WhatsApp +6012 332 1875, or use our contact page.

This page summarises our approach to confidentiality. It is not itself a contract. Binding obligations are set out in a signed NDA or engagement agreement.
