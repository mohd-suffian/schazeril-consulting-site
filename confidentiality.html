Confidentiality & NDA

What you share with us stays with us.

Our commitment

Senior leaders bring us sensitive questions. We treat them that way.

Acquisitions, listings, restructuring and leadership decisions are often confidential long before they are public. Every conversation with us is treated as confidential from the first contact, whether or not an NDA is in place.

  • Confidential from the first conversation
  • Mutual NDA available before detailed discussion
  • No client names or details shared without consent
  • Information used only to serve you

Non-disclosure agreements

We are happy to sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share detailed or commercially sensitive information. We can use our standard mutual NDA or review yours.

Our standard mutual NDA covers:

  • Confidential information: all business, financial, strategic, organisational and personal information shared by either party, in any form.
  • Purpose: information is used only to discuss or deliver the engagement.
  • Non-disclosure: neither party discloses the other's information to third parties, except to advisers bound by confidentiality or where the law requires.
  • Protection: each party protects the other's information with at least the care it gives its own.
  • Return or destruction: on request, information is returned or securely destroyed.
  • Duration: obligations continue for the period agreed in the NDA, including after the engagement ends.

SC Executive Advisor sessions

Advisory conversations are confidential by design. We do not share what is discussed with anyone in your organisation or outside it without your consent.

Client confidentiality

We do not name clients, publish case studies or use client logos without written permission.

To request our standard mutual NDA, or to send yours for review, contact us at +6012 332 1875 or through our contact page.
